Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: January 2025

1. Not Medical Advice

The content on this website — including procedure descriptions, price ranges, and package details — is provided for general informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. It is not a substitute for a professional medical evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment recommendation from a qualified physician.

2. We Are a Coordination Service, Not a Healthcare Provider

VietMed Tour connects patients with independent, accredited hospitals and specialists in Vietnam and coordinates supporting logistics. We are not a hospital, clinic, or medical practice, and we do not employ the physicians who provide treatment. All diagnoses, treatment recommendations, and medical procedures are the sole responsibility of the treating hospital and physician.

3. No Guarantee of Results

Every patient and procedure is different. Prices, timelines, and outcomes described on this website are typical ranges based on general information and are not guaranteed. Your actual quote, treatment plan, and results will be determined by your treating physician following a proper medical assessment.

4. Treatment Decisions Rest With You and Your Physician

Any decision to proceed with a consultation, screening, or procedure is yours to make, ideally in consultation with your treating physician and, where appropriate, your personal doctor at home. You should disclose your full medical history and ask questions about risks, benefits, and alternatives before consenting to any treatment.

5. Emergency Situations

This website is not intended for use in a medical emergency. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.

6. Pricing & Availability

Price ranges shown on this website reflect typical market rates and are subject to change based on your specific condition, the facility and surgeon selected, and other factors determined during your medical assessment. Always request an itemized quote before confirming treatment.

7. Consult Your Own Physician

Before traveling for treatment, we recommend consulting your personal physician about your fitness to travel, any pre-existing conditions, and how your treatment abroad may affect your ongoing care at home.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this disclaimer, please reach out through our contact page.
