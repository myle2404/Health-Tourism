FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about safety, cost, procedures, and travel logistics before planning your medical trip to Vietnam.

Getting started

Tell us what you're looking for, and we match you with an accredited partner hospital and specialist for your case. You'll receive a fully itemized quote, and our coordination team handles appointments, transportation, and support from your first inquiry through your return home.

Your initial consultation and quote are free, with no obligation. You only pay for the treatment and services you choose — all itemized up front, with no hidden coordination fees added on top.

Use the contact form on any service page or the homepage to share your treatment interest and a few details. Our team typically responds within 24 hours with a detailed, itemized cost breakdown.

Safety & accreditation

Yes. We partner only with hospitals holding JCI, ISO, and Vietnamese MOH accreditations — the same standards applied to leading hospitals in the US, UK, and Australia. Vietnam is home to several JCI-accredited facilities, reflecting its growing status as a world-class medical tourism destination.

Our partner specialists are trained internationally — including in the US, UK, Australia, and South Korea — and many maintain affiliations with international medical associations. All consultations and records are provided in fluent English.

Partner hospitals follow the same clinical protocols as accredited facilities worldwide, with full emergency and follow-up care available. Because standard travel insurance typically excludes elective procedures, we recommend a specialist medical travel policy that covers complications, revision care, and medical repatriation — our team can point you to suitable options.

Costs & payment

Most patients save 40–90% compared to US, UK, or Australian prices. For example, a comprehensive health checkup that costs $8,000–$15,000 in the US typically runs $500–$1,500 in Vietnam for the same level of testing and care, and procedures like dental implants or cosmetic surgery see similar reductions.

Every quote is fully itemized — covering treatment, facility and anesthesia fees where applicable, and any additional services you choose such as accommodation, transportation, translation, and aftercare. We recommend confirming exactly what's included before booking, and we make that easy with a transparent, single price list.

Lower pricing largely reflects lower operating costs, staff wages, and clinic overheads — not lower standards. Many partner surgeons and specialists trained internationally and use the same modern equipment found in Western hospitals.

Health screening & procedures

Most comprehensive health checkups are completed in a single day. Full, English-language digital clinical reports are typically delivered within 24–48 hours, and appointments can usually be booked within the same week of your request.

Packages commonly include a full blood panel, imaging (such as MRI or CT scan), cancer marker screening, ECG, ultrasound, dental, eye and ENT exams, and a specialist consultation — see our Health Screening page for tier-by-tier details.

Recovery time depends on the specific procedure — non-invasive treatments often need little to no downtime, while surgical procedures may require several days to a few weeks. Your personalized quote and treatment plan will outline the expected recovery timeline for your case.

Travel & logistics

Most international patients need a visa to enter Vietnam. Our team provides supporting documentation from your chosen hospital and can help guide you through the application process.

Our coordination team can arrange airport pickup, accommodation, hospital transfers, interpretation, and day-to-day support — all coordinated so you can focus on your treatment and recovery, not logistics.

It depends on your treatment. A health checkup can often be completed in a 3–5 day visit, while surgical procedures typically require a longer stay to allow for pre-op consultation, the procedure itself, and initial recovery before you're cleared to fly home.
