Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

VietMed Tour ("we," "our," or "us") helps international patients connect with accredited hospitals and specialists in Vietnam. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number, and country of residence
  • Information about the treatment or service you're interested in, and any health-related details you voluntarily share to help us prepare your quote
  • Messages you send through our contact and quote request forms

We may also automatically collect limited technical information, such as browser type and general usage patterns, to help us understand how our website is used.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Prepare and send you a personalized quote or consultation response
  • Coordinate appointments, travel, and support services on your behalf
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve our website and services

4. Sharing With Partner Hospitals & Providers

To arrange your treatment, we share relevant information with the partner hospital, clinic, or specialist you choose to work with, and with service providers involved in your trip (such as accommodation or transportation partners), only as needed to deliver the services you've requested. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us. You may also opt out of non-essential communications from us at any time.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults arranging medical care for themselves or on behalf of a minor under their care. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out through our contact page.
