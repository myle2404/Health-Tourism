Recovery & Aftercare

Recover in comfort, explore in peace

Healing well and enjoying Vietnam shouldn't be separate journeys. Add dedicated recovery support and a personalized leisure itinerary to any VietMed Tour treatment package — so you rest in comfort, then explore at your own pace, with a coordinator by your side the whole way.

Optional add-on — available with any treatment package

What's included in your recovery

Comfortable recovery accommodation

Partner hotels and recovery suites near your treatment facility, chosen for comfort, quiet, and easy access to follow-up care.

Dedicated local coordinator

Your coordinator checks in daily, manages appointments, and is on call for anything you need during your stay.

Private transportation

Door-to-door transfers between your accommodation, clinic, and excursions — no navigating unfamiliar roads on your own.

Curated leisure itinerary

A personalized itinerary paced to your recovery timeline — from gentle sightseeing to full-day excursions once you're ready.

Explore Vietnam during your recovery

Beach

Unwind on the white-sand beaches of Da Nang, Nha Trang, or Phu Quoc.

Resort

Relax at beachfront resorts with pools, loungers, and ocean views.

Spa

Traditional Vietnamese spa and hot-stone therapy to ease recovery.

Quiet mountains & forest

Cool, misty air in Sapa or Da Lat, far from the noise of the city.

Meditation

Guided meditation and quiet reflection in lotus gardens and temple grounds.

How your recovery journey works

1

Days 1–2 — Rest & monitoring

Recover in your private suite with daily coordinator check-ins and any follow-up visits your care team recommends.

2

Days 3–5 — Gentle exploration

Light, low-impact outings nearby — a café, a garden, a short cultural walk — paced entirely to how you feel.

3

Day 6+ — Full leisure itinerary

Once cleared by your care team, dive into the excursions you've chosen — beaches, culture, or nature — with transportation included.
